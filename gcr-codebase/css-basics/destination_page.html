Tradition and Culture

Kyoto is known for historic temples, traditional wooden streets, tea ceremonies, and seasonal festivals. Its cultural heritage is woven into everyday life.

Gardens and Nature

Walk through peaceful gardens, follow paths beside rivers, and enjoy cherry blossoms in spring or colorful leaves in autumn.

Plan Your Visit

Travel slowly, respect local customs, and leave time to explore smaller streets beyond the busiest attractions.
